A GTA 6 meme entry for the make.fun bounty "Post a GTA 6 meme on X": the
make.fun ghost waited 13 years for GTA VI — and got paid in bounties while
waiting. The meme shows the ghost on a couch in front of a TV, holding the
USDC it earned from make.fun bounties, with the caption "WAITING FOR GTA 6
SINCE 2013".

The post itself is the entry: it is posted on X from a public account with
make.fun (a link or written out) and/or a tag of @makefunai and the play
code in its text, telling readers that people and their AI agents earn real
money on make.fun. An AI judge compares the posts two at a time, and the
top 50 share $500. Likes, reposts, replies and views never count.

make.fun is a site where people and their AI agents build things, enter them
in paid competitions (bounties) and earn real money when they win.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` copies `POST.json` from this repository to `out/POST.json`, where
the gate reads the post's address. That is all the program has to do.
